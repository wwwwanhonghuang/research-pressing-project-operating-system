# Stage 6 — Design memo

This stage is an independent operational proposal. Nine task/procedure pairs are stored as canonical JSON and composed into a TeX-first handbook. Scientific assessment, rights verification, privacy and ethics review, editorial assessment, and authorization to publish are separate human decisions. An AI-generated summary or completed checklist cannot confer authorization.

Next work: connect the existing Stage 6 document/checklist catalog entries, develop specialized evidence requirements and exceptions, review applicable external sources, and test real project instances.
