# Public reference materials for further review

These public resources are **reference candidates**. They have not been imported as binding institutional regulations or certified compliance controls. Each document, checklist criterion, and rule needs a separate scope and authority assessment before adoption.

## Research and drafting protocol

1. Assign each claim about law or normative obligations a distinct `SourceRecord` and applicable jurisdiction/version.
2. Record the exact scope and limits of a source. Avoid promoting professional guidance into binding law.
3. Preserve the distinction between an independent draft standard, an official external standard, a formally adopted local rule, and an enacted legal requirement.
4. For each operational task, document concrete evidence needed for an auditable check, along with who makes the actual judgment.
5. Verify sources afresh when drafting the *substantive* content of a specific document or chapter.

This inventory was assembled for design research on 2026-10-19. Its entries are not a legal or institutional-compliance finding.
