# Human Accountability and Evidence — Prototype Rules

1. **Traceability.** Claims, quotations, figures and material edits should be linkable to sources, reviewers and version identifiers.
2. **Authority separation.** Clearly distinguish applicable law, publisher or institutional policy (only where verified), professional guidance, and project-proposed standards.
3. **Reviews and approvals.** A completion checkbox is evidence of a check, not authorization. Named human reviewers approve decisions where required. Publication requires an explicit human authorization record.
4. **AI assistance.** Models can propose questions, summaries, edits, risk flags and draft reports. Output must be reviewed and must not claim a verified fact, consent, rights clearance or approval automatically.
5. **Sensitive material.** Access controls, consent, privacy, research embargoes and safe AI input handling must be established before operational use with real data. This file-only prototype has **no access-control enforcement or secure record store**.
6. **Corrections and history.** Preserve references to the originally authorized version, correction reasons and timestamps; avoid silently rewriting completed-project audit trails.
7. **Automation boundaries.** Scripts may create directories and validate shapes. They cannot establish that factual, legal, scientific or ethical conditions have been satisfied.
